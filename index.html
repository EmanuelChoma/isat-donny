Love one another. Grow together.

Ratanang Community Center is a non-profit, community-based organisation. We run a feeding scheme, an agriculture garden, aftercare for children, free counselling, and a donation programme that funds it all. On this site you can make a donation and choose the programme you want to support.

How your donation works

1. Pick a programme. 2. Choose a once-off, monthly or yearly gift. 3. Receive a thank-you and, if you wish, a Section 18A tax certificate.

Every rand is recorded against your donor profile, so you always know where it went.

Our five programmes

Donation

Aftercare

Counselling

Free confidential counselling for youth, families and adults.

Agriculture

Community garden that grows vegetables for the kitchen and for sale.

Feeding Scheme
